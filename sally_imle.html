Who sets the exam

  • The exam is set and run by the Examinations Committee for Internship & Licensing of the Scientific Council of the Israel Medical Association (IMA / הר"י), on behalf of the Ministry of Health.
  • Committee chair: Dr. Aziz Daraushe (ד"ר דראושה עזיז) — internal medicine / cardiology / emergency medicine.
  • The Supreme Examinations Committee has 25 members + chair: 10 IMA reps, 10 from the scientific (specialty) associations, plus hospital, community-clinic and resident reps. The chairs of the Internal & Surgical Boards also join. Questions come from the specialty associations, so expect textbook-anchored, guideline-based single-best-answer items.
  • Appeals are decided by a separate appeals committee, strictly per the recommended textbook edition.

High-yield patterns seen in recent papers

  • Surgery: bowel obstruction (adhesions), diverticular disease & when to operate, hernia anatomy (femoral, Spigelian), breast cancer surgery & risk factors, pancreatitis, hepatobiliary (FNH, PSC→cholangiocarcinoma), bariatric indications, wound healing (first cell = neutrophil), ATLS/damage-control & hemorrhagic shock classes, melanoma ABCDE.
  • Pediatrics: neonatology (NEC, TORCH — CMV, inborn errors like MSUD/MCADD), cardiology (ASD/VSD/coarctation from cath data, myocarditis, rheumatic fever), nephrology (PSGN biopsy criteria, HUS, relapsing nephrotic), heme (ITP, Fanconi anemia, iron-deficiency from cow's milk), orthopedics (SCFE, Osgood-Schlatter, transient synovitis), Guillain-Barré, febrile seizure, ADEM, Kawasaki, genetic syndromes (Turner, DiGeorge/22q11, Down, ataxia-telangiectasia), toxicology (caustics, CCB overdose), SIDS/BRUE, croup vs epiglottitis.
  • Internal medicine: MODY vs T2DM, drug interactions (verapamil→digoxin, allopurinol+azathioprine), hemolysis/PNH & blood smears, mesothelioma (asbestos), Lemierre (Fusobacterium), ITP first-line therapy side effects, HFrEF four pillars & CRT, landmark trials (SPRINT, ACCOMPLISH, ALLHAT), COPD steroid harms, gout treatment pitfalls.
  • OB-GYN: preeclampsia/eclampsia & MgSO4, PPH (atony), placenta previa vs abruption, ectopic, CTG interpretation, cervical screening.
  • Psychiatry: NMS vs serotonin syndrome, MDD/bipolar/schizophrenia criteria, and Israeli law — the District Psychiatrist and involuntary hospitalization.
